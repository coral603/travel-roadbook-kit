# Intake questionnaire

Ask only questions that can change the route. Consolidate them into one message.

## Essential

1. Exact arrival and departure dates, times, airports, and fixed tickets.
2. Number of travellers, age range, mobility, and relevant health constraints.
3. Number of drivers, transmission preference, and acceptable daily driving load.
4. Luggage count and size.
5. Must-do experiences and explicit exclusions.
6. Comfortable hiking level and maximum duration/elevation.
7. Total or per-person budget and included categories.
8. Fixed bookings, non-refundable items, and required cities.
9. Preferred pace and willingness to use flights, trains, ferries, or one-way rentals.
10. Output needs: decision brief, roadbook, offline copy, PDF, or visa documents.

## Useful only when relevant

- Food restrictions or self-catering preference.
- Photography timing, wildlife, diving, skiing, or other specialist activities.
- Need for child seats, accessible rooms, or special vehicles.
- Passport/visa constraints that affect transit countries.
- Comfort with early departures, night driving, exposed roads, or ferries.

## Intake rules

- Transcribe supplied tickets once and ask the user to confirm unclear details.
- Do not ask for passport numbers, payment-card data, or complete booking PINs.
- Do not ask questions whose answers cannot change the plan.
- State assumptions explicitly when proceeding without a non-critical answer.
