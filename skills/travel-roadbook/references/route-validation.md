# Route validation

## Source hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. operator or government source;
2. official tourism or road authority;
3. real routing service;
4. established booking platform;
5. recent traveller reports for qualitative context only.

Use current web research for schedules, prices, opening hours, policies, and seasonal access. Record the check date.

## Validate a self-drive day

- Route every mandatory waypoint in the intended order.
- Add stops, fuel/charging, meals, ferries, parking, and photo time.
- Compare pure driving time with total day duration.
- Check road class, mountain passes, tolls, seasonal gates, and expected daylight.
- Confirm the driver and vehicle can return or check in before closing.
- Keep a weather-safe alternative for exposed hikes and scenic roads.

Do not multiply map time by a universal factor. Urban traffic, ferries, narrow roads, winter conditions, and photo stops behave differently.

## Validate public transport

- Check the exact travel date, not a generic weekly timetable.
- Confirm airport/station/port names and direction.
- Calculate door-to-door time, including check-in and transfer.
- Treat separate tickets as unprotected connections.
- Check baggage allowance and last-mile transport.

## Evidence log

For each critical claim, store:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Checked on | 2026-07-31 |
| Leg | City A → City B |
| Source | Official operator URL |
| Confirmed | Departure operates on selected date |
| Uncertain | Winter schedule not yet released |
| Action | Recheck 90 days before travel |

Screenshots are supplementary evidence, not a substitute for a text link and date.
