# Roadbook QA checklist

## Content

- All dates match the fixed international itinerary.
- Each day ends at the following night's accommodation.
- Transport legs form a continuous chain.
- Local and translated place names are paired consistently.
- Daily images, if used, depict that day's primary location.
- Difficult days state realistic total duration and fallback.
- No outdated option remains after a decision is confirmed.

## Interaction

- Sticky navigation works after scrolling.
- Scroll position and click both update the active nav item.
- Anchors are not hidden under the sticky header.
- External links are visible as text and usable without QR codes.
- Back-to-top and print controls work.
- Keyboard focus is visible.

## Mobile

- Test at 360 px and 390 px width.
- No horizontal overflow.
- Main text is at least 16 px.
- Tap targets are at least 44 px high.
- Tables collapse or scroll without clipping.
- Important information is not conveyed by colour alone.

## Print/PDF

- Use an A4 print preview.
- Hide interactive-only controls.
- Avoid splitting day headings from their first content.
- Avoid cards cut across pages where possible.
- Verify links remain selectable.
- Inspect every rendered page, not only the first.

## Final consistency

Run the validator and privacy scanner. Open every route link. Compare the final output with tickets and the booking register one last time.
